## What is blocked and what can proceed

**Blocked:** T-008 at S3 (S1/S2 evidence complete, committed at `f42f8fd`). **Every remaining task

## Options

- **A. Restore the link, verify, re-measure Stage 4 as a new gated task (recommended).** Zack runs
  read-only diagnostics (`nvidia-smi -q -d PCI`, `sudo lspci -vv -s <gpu> | grep -i lnksta`,
  `sudo dmesg | grep -iE 'pcie|aer|link'` — proposed, not run by the executor), restores Gen4 x8
  (reseat/BIOS check if needed), verifies under load, then a new card re-runs the Stage 4 session
  and T-008's S2 check. **Cost:** Zack's diagnostic time + ~52 min GPU session (38.3 + 13.6 measured)
  + gates. **Risk:** the link may not restore without hardware intervention; if Gen4 is no longer
  sustainable, option C becomes the question.
- **B. Accept Stage 4 with a recorded S2-FAIL deviation.** Release carries Stage 3 as the baseline
  and documents Stage 4 as a failed reproducibility check on a Gen3 machine. **Cost:** one decision
  line + report caveats. **Risk:** S2's purpose is unmet; the machine change is unexplained and may
  recur or worsen; every later stage inherits the doubt.
- **C. Treat Gen3 as the machine's new state and re-baseline.** Re-run Stage 3 64K (and arguably
  32K/128K) plus Stage 4 on the current machine. **Cost:** hours of GPU time, new gates, rewritten
  baseline numbers. Only sensible if the link cannot be restored.

## Recommendation

**A, high confidence** that the PCIe change is real and material (the telemetry is unambiguous:
every Stage 4 row Gen3, every Stage 3 row Gen4, same clocks, lower power); **medium confidence**
that it fully explains the S2 deltas — the re-measure after restoration is exactly the test. Fold
a cross-session canary-baseline check into T-011's methodology work regardless of the decision.
