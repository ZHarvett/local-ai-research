## Why it cannot proceed

`wf.sh done T-011` will refuse while the evidence entry is a directory. This is the same
card/kit mismatch ruled on in E-010 (T-009): "The kit takes files as evidence", and the E-010
rule (`docs/decisions.md`, DECISION E-010): "A card and the kit must agree on what acceptance
can run and what evidence is; when they do not, the card is amended by the planning chat,
never worked around by the executor." The executor may not edit existing cards or acceptance
criteria (docs/WORKFLOW.md, Plan edit rights), and T-011's decision rights cover only the
wording of the drafts and methodology.md. A kit change is outside T-011's files_write and
would repeat the option E-010 rejected.

## Options

- **A. Amend T-011's evidence to files** (the E-010 resolution): `docs/plans/step-0-baseline.md,
  docs/upstream/llama-cpp-mtp-draft2-repeated-phrase.md,

  hashing rule). Fixes the class of mismatch for all future cards, but changes the tooling

  unmerged, the v1.9 plan and the drafts stay off main, and T-012–T-016 (walkthrough, scrub,
  completion report, tag, publish) wait; no benefit over A.
