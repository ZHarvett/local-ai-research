## Why it cannot proceed

- E-010 is open and its DECISION file exists (~/Downloads/DECISION-E-010.md) but cannot be
  recorded while T-009 is not blocked-decision; the ruling's exception does not cover the
  actual outcome, and the driver must not improvise kit state changes.
- Two open escalations on one task create an ordering trap: `cmd_decision` unblocks the
  task after recording, so recording E-010 first leaves E-011 unrecordable (task no longer
  blocked), and vice versa — unless the task is re-blocked between the two recordings
  (`wf.sh block` is legal from any non-terminal status; "ready" is a legal unblock target
  in TRANSITIONS["blocked-decision"], so the restore is clean).
- The wiped steps_done is a second, independent hazard: T-009 was ready with no steps_done,
  so any executor (/continue or wf-loop) would start at S1 and overwrite
  docs/recon/T-009-start.txt (currently c24f410…) — explicitly forbidden by the ruling and
  by DECISION E-010's Scope ("S1 and S2 are already committed and stand: do not re-run them
  and do not overwrite docs/recon/T-009-start.txt; if the amend resets T-009 to todo,
  resume from S3"). `wf.sh step-done` only records steps for ACTIVE tasks
  (claimed/in-progress/awaiting-gate; scripts/_wf.py:1582), so S1/S2 cannot be restored
  while T-009 is ready or blocked — it must be claimed+started first.
- No wf-loop is running and no other session is active, so nothing will pick T-009 up
