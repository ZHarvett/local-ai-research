## What happened

Stage 4 run 2 (session `stage4-run2`, 2026-11-14, stock-64K + tuned-64K, 5 repetitions) is the S2
reproducibility run under rule R5; its gate passed a–f before and after one clean `--rerun-flagged`
pass. Rule R7 (declared 2026-11-14, `docs/decisions.md`): "Every gated GPU session checks the link
under load with `nvidia-smi --query-gpu=pcie.link.gen.current,pcie.link.width.current
--format=csv,noheader`. Anything other than '4, 8' while a server is generating stops the session
and escalates; it is never averaged into the data."

1. At poll 3 (14:55:43Z) five 1-second link samples read **4,8 / 2,8 / 2,8 / 1,8 / 4,8** while the
   session was running (canary-end cell); utilization.gpu 95% was queried only *after* the five
   samples. The executor took a 30-sample burst at 2 Hz with per-sample utilization
   (14:56:52–14:57:07Z): every sample at util ≥ 38% read 4,8; the downshift to 2,8 → 1,8 happened
   only at 0% utilization as power decayed. The executor concluded "documented idle downshift in an
   idle gap between canary requests", judged R7's stop condition not met, and continued — including
   into the S4 re-run pass — **without an escalation brief**. The conditions-log entry
   (`docs/stage4-run2-conditions.md`, "R7 investigation") labels itself "executor judgement, flagged
   for the post-review" and names itself as the escalation point.
2. The cycle-1 review (`reviews/T-021/r1-REVIEW_REPORT.md`, finding **F-2, MAJOR, DECIDE**)
   re-derived from the committed telemetry (`data/raw/stage4-run2/telemetry/*.csv.gz`, 0.5 s
   cadence, 4,091 rows); this session independently reproduced every figure:
   - Sustained generation (P-state P1 ∧ power ≥ 50 W ∧ gr clock ≥ 2500 MHz): **3,556 samples,
     0 non-(4,8)**.
   - **Every sample of the three measured cell instances is Gen4 x8**: stock-64K-i0 1,544 rows,
     stock-64K-i1 342, tuned-64K-i0 771 = 2,657 rows, no exception at any utilization.
   - Poll 3's exact sequence (4, 2, 2, 1, 4 at 1 s) is reproduced from canary-end telemetry showing
     util 0.0 throughout 14:55:41.474–14:55:46.476Z — the idle-gap inference is machine-confirmed.
   - New evidence the executor did not have: **exactly two rows session-wide with util > 0 and
     gen ≠ 4**, both in *re-run canary* cells, both idle→load ramps:
     `canary-rerun-start` 15:01:21.726Z (util 94%, gen 2, width 8, 9.92 W, P5, 1012 MHz; the four
     preceding rows util 0 with power decaying; the next 0.5 s sample gen 4, 28.5 W, P1, 2820 MHz,
     util 96%) and `canary-rerun-end` 15:07:42.999Z (util 26%, gen 2, width 8, 9.77 W, P5, 577 MHz;
     previous row util 0 / gen 2 / 10.12 W; next sample gen 4, 32.6 W, P1, util 96%).
     `utilization.gpu` is a trailing average: it reads high while power, clock and P-state still

3. Under a **strict** reading of "while a server is generating" (any kernel activity), those two
   rows violate R7 and the session should have stopped and escalated. Under the **under-load**
   reading they are transition artefacts, like the idle downshifts. Neither reading touches a
   measured window: stock-64K and tuned-64K are Gen4 x8 in every row. Canaries are link/health
   checks, not S2 data.

## Why it cannot proceed

- The card's `decision_rights.must_escalate` lists "any stop rule firing, **including R7**"; shared
  rule 7 says escalate anything unexpected. The executor's inline resolution — transparent and
  substantively supported, but still a decision the card reserves — is the process deviation F-2
  names. DECIDE findings are never resolved inline (the kit refuses any other disposition).
- Acceptance item A5's link clause ("the link read 4, 8 at every sample") stays **ESCALATE** until
  this is ruled on. The wording is stronger than the log's own claim ("every confirmed-generation
  sample"); whichever option is chosen, the ruling must state the scope so the cycle-2 reviewer can
  bring wording and evidence into line (the card itself is frozen; a wording change would need a
  plan amendment).
- R6 makes the interpretation consequential: "Run 2 is the only re-run … no run 3 without a new
  decision." The reviewer orders **no RE-MEASURE**.

## Options

- **A. Ratify the under-load reading (reviewer recommendation).** R7 applies to sustained
  generation. Run 2 stands as the S2 run. Record the ruling as a `docs/decisions.md` line; the
  executor adds the telemetry verification and the two ramp rows to the conditions log, and records
  that the executor should have escalated the ambiguity even though its conclusion was correct.
  T-008 proceeds. Cost: one decision line plus doc edits (minutes, no GPU).
- **B. Strict reading: any kernel activity counts.** The two re-run-canary samples violate R7; the
  executor's continuation was a stop-rule breach; R6 bars run 3 without a new decision. The planning
  chat would still have to decide whether run 2's S2 data stands with a recorded deviation, since
  every measured-cell sample is Gen4 x8. Cost: a decision, and possibly a new gated task — if a
  re-measure were approved, redo T-021 S1–S5 (about 45 min gated GPU: 26 min session + 9 min re-run
  + analysis), fresh decision first per R6; the old run is superseded, never deleted.

## Recommendation

Option **A** — the reviewer's recommendation, on stronger evidence than the executor had: all 3,556
sustained-generation samples and all 2,657 measured-cell samples are Gen4 x8; the only util>0
non-Gen4 rows are two 0.5 s idle→load ramps in canary cells; poll 3's sequence is machine-confirmed
as an idle gap. R7's purpose — protecting measurement validity from the Gen3 fault that invalidated
run 1 — is fully satisfied. The process deviation (no escalation brief at poll 3) is recorded, not
repeated. Confidence: **high**.
